1983Fulbari

Entry into Infrastructure Supply

S N Polymers began its journey with the supply of concrete pipes in Birpara, through its sister company S N Precast, establishing its presence in the infrastructure sector and building the foundation for its future involvement in water supply projects associated with the Minor Irrigation Department.

02
1988Fulbari

Expansion into PVC Pipe Supply

The company expanded its product portfolio to include PVC pipes, strengthening its involvement in water infrastructure and broadening its capabilities to serve evolving project requirements.

03
2004Darjeeling

Hydraulic Ram Systems

S N Polymers diversified into hydraulic ram systems, applying water-powered lifting technology to locations where elevation differences and challenging terrain made water distribution more complex.

04
2008Jalpaiguri

Sprinkler Irrigation Systems

The company entered sprinkler-based irrigation, extending its work into agricultural water management and providing systems designed to distribute water efficiently across cultivated areas.

Specialisation

2016Darjeeling

ODEX Drilling & High-Yield Tubewells

S N Polymers adopted the ODEX drilling method for groundwater development in challenging geological conditions. The project included drilling of 61 high-yield tubewells in the Himalayan foothills, reflecting the company's experience in specialised drilling and groundwater infrastructure.

61 high-yield tubewells

2018–22Darjeeling

Rainwater Harvesting

The company undertook a rainwater harvesting initiative focused on improving drinking water access and strengthening local water security. The project benefited approximately 750 households and was completed in 2022.

2021Malda

Solar-Powered Pumping Systems

S N Polymers completed the installation of 100 solar-powered pumps, each rated at 5 HP, expanding its capabilities into renewable-energy-based water pumping and demonstrating experience in coordinating multiple installations.

2021Malda

Drinking Water Systems for Madrasahs

The company delivered drinking water infrastructure across 49 high Madrasahs, incorporating tubewells and filtration plants. The project supported access to drinking water in educational institutions through the installation of essential water infrastructure.

49 high Madrasahs

School Water Supply Project

S N Polymers undertook a water supply project covering 200 schools, involving deep tubewell boring and submersible pumping systems. The multi-location scope required coordinated installation and execution to address the water supply needs of educational institutions.

200 schools
